### Summary

The coverage matrix and the audit and rate-limit designs hold up, and the intentional requirement refinements are well flagged. The approval handshake is not yet sound: stdio-mode visibility of the code is undefined (R-01), the socket does not itself require a human (R-02), and the socket is under-specified (R-03). The failed-login throttle is bypassable and unbounded (R-04), and the redactor rule would erase the design's own correlation IDs (R-05). Five Major findings exceed the allowed two, so the verdict is NOT-READY.

### Summary

The stage is broadly well-structured: the IDs trace completely and most rows are measurable. The 5-client and no-real-clock tensions are handled sensibly. It is NOT-READY because four security-design gaps would force guessing at design: the single token versus per-client identity contradiction (R-01), the untestable agent-cannot-approve property (R-02), the undefined ordering of audit and write for the fail-closed guarantee (R-03), and missing Host and Origin protection for HTTP mode (R-04). The Minor items can be fixed in the same revision.
